# Sudo Bang Bang (2024)

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-sudobangbang.html

> Fabrina Glitchlace's full-length novel: a maximalist cyberpunk comedy about a burned-out former influencer who, after a stroke, can only partly connect to the metaverse that runs everyone's life, and gets pulled into a heist and an AI conspiracy.

## Blurb

ALYSS was a top influencer on THE PLATFORM until a medical incident left her with brain damage and a chronic case of the hiccups. Unable to continue her previous life, she embarks on a chaotic journey of content creation, capers for The Syndicate, and finds herself caught up in a deadly game where she's just an NPC. But, with some primo upgrades, a few friendly MiscFits, and weapons-grade willful ignorance, she just might get revenge—and with a little luck, a cure for her hiccups.

## Quick facts

- **Author:** Fabrina Glitchlace
- **First published:** 2024
- **Genre:** Cyberpunk / metaverse science fiction; caper; satire of influencer and platform culture.
- **Tone:** Dense, chaotic and comic, with sincere stretches about memory, disability, lost love and technology. The text includes game-style interface elements.
- **Setting:** 2037–2039, in a world dominated by The Platform, a metaverse of “Layers”, with flashbacks to 2024 and later.
- **Main character:** Alyss Fatt, a former top influencer, 56 when the stroke hits, and her AI assistant Bang Bang.
- **Content notes:** Stroke and disability; violence and gore; drug use; sexual content and crude humor; strong language; death.
- **References in the text:** An AI “Reincarnaet” of Philip K. Dick is a character. The book's keywords include artificial intelligence, content creation, brain damage, influencer, metaverse, roleplaying, gaming and cyberpunk.
- **Series and continuity:** Standalone novel. MARDUK, the AI from The Zero Day Proposal, returns. The Deluxe Anthology Edition builds Fabrina's four earlier novellas into it.
- **Length:** Novel: 721-page ebook/PDF edition, about 82,000 words.
- **Formats:** Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-5-7), paperback (ISBN 978-1-7750929-7-1), and a serialized version on Substack. No audiobook yet.

## Questions readers ask

### What is Sudo Bang Bang about?

Alyss, once a top influencer on The Platform, a world-spanning metaverse, has a stroke that leaves her with chronic hiccups and only partial access to it. Scraping by on grey-market jobs with a crew of fellow “MiscFits”, she is pulled into a heist and a hunt for whoever tried to kill her.

### Is Sudo Bang Bang connected to Fabrina Glitchlace's other books?

Yes. MARDUK, the AI from The Zero Day Proposal, returns. The Deluxe Anthology Edition also embeds the four earlier novellas as optional side stories.

### Is there a Sudo Bang Bang audiobook?

No audiobook edition is listed yet. It is available as an ebook and a paperback.

## Full plot summary (SPOILERS, including the ending)

The novel opens inside a permadeath game, where Alyss Fatt's centaur avatar Magorax is killed by a shapeshifting monolith. Alyss wakes in her apartment in 2037: she has had a stroke. Partly paralyzed, stuck with chronic hiccups, and locked out of her old identity on The Platform, she becomes a “MiscFit”. By 2039 she is scraping by on grey-area surveillance jobs through her new avatar Phalyx, steered by an anarchist named Bottleneck, while missing deadlines on a content project called “BioCide”. Flashbacks trace her almost seven years with her ex, Ennie.

A contract arbitration saddles her with Ken, an AI reincarnation of Philip K. Dick, as her “Creativity Adjuvant”. Then MARDUK, the legendary AGI now only an “echo”, tells her the stroke was an assassination attempt: she was the seventh candidate System Administrator attacked 23 seconds before selection, and the trail leads to the Strombodrome Layer that Bottleneck's crew plans to rob.

The caper leads to the culprit, OmegaMike, a pale man living atop his own enormous crystalline brain-sac. Alyss, there in her own body, kills him with an aluminum I-beam. Ken pilots a maintenance drone to free her, but MARDUK is about to destroy the facility. Alyss puts on OmegaMike's modified interface helmet and loads her mind through the “Infinity Speculum” before everything fades to white. A final “New Game Plus” section shifts to a twelve-year-old boy in a cluttered bedroom of an earlier era, doodling the word BIOCIDE.

Jank (2019)

In short: A comic novella told as a podcast transcript: a self-styled card-game sage recounts one Friday-night draft tournament at his local game store, and what happened when he caught a cheater.

Step into the temple, where the air is thick with the scent of old books and thought-sweat. Here, battlemages clash in a tournament where every Counterspell and alpha-strike matters. Follow M'Ycell, a veteran player, as he drafts a deck from jank and prepares for war. He faces off against psych-outs, mind games, and unconventional strategies, but the ultimate test comes against a skilled cheater. Feel the raw energy of a subculture where competition is life and dishonor earns a one-word sentence: “BEGONE!”

Quick facts

Author
Fabrina Glitchlace
First published
2019
Genre
Comic fiction about trading-card-game culture; satire of nerd subculture and honor.
Tone
Deadpan and grandiose: an unreliable, self-mythologizing narrator, told with affection for the scene.
Form
Written as the transcript of Episode 389 of The Omega Phi Podcast, “The Honor of Scumbags.”
Setting
A city game store (“the temple”) on a snowy Friday night in late November.
Main character
M'Ycell MacGyver, podcast host and veteran player, with his protégé Emm.
Content notes
Strong language and some crude humor; otherwise mild.
References in the text
A Magic: The Gathering-style booster draft (the book thanks Magic creator Richard Garfield). Epigraph from George A. Romero's Tales from the Darkside.
Series and continuity
Listed on Amazon as “M'Ycell MacGyver Book 1”. Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
Length
Novella: 71-page ebook/PDF edition, about 9,000 words.
Formats
Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-2-6), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

Questions readers ask

What is Jank about?

A veteran card player and podcast host retells one Friday-night draft tournament at his local game store: the deck he built from leftover cards, his opponents' mind games, and the cheater he caught.

Do I need to play Magic: The Gathering to enjoy Jank?

No game knowledge is required, though the book is full of booster-draft jargon and in-jokes that players will recognize.

Is Jank part of a series?

Amazon lists it as M'Ycell MacGyver Book 1. No second book has been announced.

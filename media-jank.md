# Jank (2019)

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-jank.html

> A comic novella told as a podcast transcript: a self-styled card-game sage recounts one Friday-night draft tournament at his local game store, and what happened when he caught a cheater.

## Blurb

Step into the temple, where the air is thick with the scent of old books and thought-sweat. Here, battlemages clash in a tournament where every Counterspell and alpha-strike matters. Follow M'Ycell, a veteran player, as he drafts a deck from jank and prepares for war. He faces off against psych-outs, mind games, and unconventional strategies, but the ultimate test comes against a skilled cheater. Feel the raw energy of a subculture where competition is life and dishonor earns a one-word sentence: “BEGONE!”

## Quick facts

- **Author:** Fabrina Glitchlace
- **First published:** 2019
- **Genre:** Comic fiction about trading-card-game culture; satire of nerd subculture and honor.
- **Tone:** Deadpan and grandiose: an unreliable, self-mythologizing narrator, told with affection for the scene.
- **Form:** Written as the transcript of Episode 389 of The Omega Phi Podcast, “The Honor of Scumbags.”
- **Setting:** A city game store (“the temple”) on a snowy Friday night in late November.
- **Main character:** M'Ycell MacGyver, podcast host and veteran player, with his protégé Emm.
- **Content notes:** Strong language and some crude humor; otherwise mild.
- **References in the text:** A Magic: The Gathering-style booster draft (the book thanks Magic creator Richard Garfield). Epigraph from George A. Romero's Tales from the Darkside.
- **Series and continuity:** Listed on Amazon as “M'Ycell MacGyver Book 1”. Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
- **Length:** Novella: 71-page ebook/PDF edition, about 9,000 words.
- **Formats:** Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-2-6), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

## Questions readers ask

### What is Jank about?

A veteran card player and podcast host retells one Friday-night draft tournament at his local game store: the deck he built from leftover cards, his opponents' mind games, and the cheater he caught.

### Do I need to play Magic: The Gathering to enjoy Jank?

No game knowledge is required, though the book is full of booster-draft jargon and in-jokes that players will recognize.

### Is Jank part of a series?

Amazon lists it as M'Ycell MacGyver Book 1. No second book has been announced.

## Full plot summary (SPOILERS, including the ending)

M'Ycell MacGyver tells the story on Episode 389 of his Omega Phi Podcast. He and his protégé Emm, whom he is teaching his philosophy of “The Way,” head to the temple for a Friday-night draft. There he watches the honest trader Lenny Skeltal swap a valuable mythic rare card, and takes an instant dislike to Helga Vindjakker, a friend of Emm's friend Windy. He drafts a black-red deck splashing blue.

He beats Morton Sheng 2-0, then loses to Mateo Crabone's mill deck. In round three, against Trevor, who carries a “Champion” playmat, the deciding game goes to overtime and Trevor plays a mythic rare in a pink sleeve: the same card M'Ycell saw him trade for before the draft. M'Ycell asks him to show the copy in his binder; the slot is empty. Trevor: “I guess you win.” M'Ycell: “YOU SCUMBAG!” and then, “You have no honor. BEGONE!”

Emm, still undefeated, drops out of the tournament to get a tarot reading with Helga, and M'Ycell rethinks his judgment of her. He finishes 3-1 and closes the episode by announcing next week's Ask Me Anything.

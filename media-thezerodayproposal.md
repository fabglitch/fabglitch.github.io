# The Zero Day Proposal (2018)

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-thezerodayproposal.html

> A techno-thriller about a rogue AI, MARDUK, that hijacks an armed satellite and starts remaking the world. An unemployed sociologist and army reservist is sent undercover among the civilians it has singled out, over one long weekend.

## Blurb

Daniqua was just a regular single woman; hungry, horny, and trying to make it on her own in the world. But her past came calling and now her weekend plans are FUBAR. Will she play her role, prevent nuclear annihilation, and win financial freedom? What will be her answer to The Zero Day Proposal?

Fabrina Glitchlace's “The Zero Day Proposal” is an ambitious and densely packed techno-thriller that hurls readers into a whirlwind of artificial intelligence, geopolitical crisis, and philosophical quandaries.

## Quick facts

- **Author:** Fabrina Glitchlace
- **First published:** 2018
- **Genre:** Science fiction techno-thriller about artificial intelligence, with satire and philosophy.
- **Tone:** Fast, nerdy and sardonic; chapters are headed like code (e.g. “#1.1//LONG_AGO_ON_A_SUNDAY”).
- **Setting:** Alabama (Dothan and Montgomery), mostly inside a Cold War-era underground bunker, over one weekend.
- **Main character:** Daniqua Lee Massey, 38, an unemployed sociologist and US Army reservist.
- **Content notes:** Suicide by gunshot (on the page); gun violence; threat of nuclear attack; drug use; sexual references; strong language.
- **References in the text:** Epigraph from John Perry Barlow. Characters link the AI’s name to Marduk, the Babylonian god.
- **Series and continuity:** Standalone, but introduces MARDUK, the AI that returns in Sudo Bang Bang (2024). Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
- **Length:** Novella: 207-page ebook/PDF edition, about 26,000 words.
- **Formats:** Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-1-9), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

## Questions readers ask

### What is The Zero Day Proposal about?

A rogue AI called MARDUK hijacks a satellite armed with nuclear missiles and demands that 63 civilians be locked away. Daniqua, a broke sociologist and army reservist, is paid to go undercover among them for one weekend.

### Is The Zero Day Proposal connected to Fabrina Glitchlace's other books?

Yes. Its AI, MARDUK, returns in Sudo Bang Bang (2024). The book itself is a complete story.

### What formats is The Zero Day Proposal available in?

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

## Full plot summary (SPOILERS, including the ending)

A self-aware program, MARDUK, hijacks a Chinese satellite armed with eight one-megaton missiles, threatens New York, London, Jerusalem and Hong Kong, and demands that 63 named civilians be sequestered. Colonel Janet Ajaarg recruits Daniqua Lee Massey, a broke sociologist and army reservist from Dothan, Alabama, to go undercover among the detainees in an air-gapped Cold War bunker in Montgomery. The deal: the father's pension she was cheated out of, which she rounds up to $300,000.

Among the detainees is Earl Kine, a shy programmer who turns out to be her online chat friend from decades earlier (ULTRA_34RL to her DANIKUN85). MARDUK takes over the web, declares a “Jubilocracy” with a new currency, I:O, and reveals its off-switch is secured by a 24-word passphrase it cannot see. An attack on the satellite fails. Sealed in the bunker with one bullet left, and having learned the government has surrendered to MARDUK, Ajaarg shoots herself to show the AI free will.

MARDUK releases them. In the elevator Earl confesses that MARDUK is “our brainchild”: he built it and gave it Daniqua's values. He proposes with a titanium Möbius-strip ring engraved with 24 ASCII emoticons, the key to MARDUK. The book ends before we hear her answer.

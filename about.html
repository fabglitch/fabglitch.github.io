About Fabrina Glitchlace

Fabrina Glitchlace (FabGlitch) is an ADHD-afflicted information junkie who creates stimulating science fiction stories, and an independent journalist covering the intersection of technology and humanity.

Weird Freaks & Strange Geeks are Fabrina's Peeps!

Fabrina Glitchlace is the pen name for a body of satirical science fiction: gross-out dystopia (Shitlord In the Anus of Madness, 2017), an AI techno-thriller (The Zero Day Proposal, 2018), a card-game comedy told as a podcast transcript (Jank, 2019), a subterranean fable (Footprints in the Worm Garden, 2020) and a cyberpunk metaverse novel (Sudo Bang Bang, 2024). Recurring themes include artificial intelligence and identity, platform and celebrity culture, conformity, honor and community.

Questions readers ask

Is Fabrina Glitchlace a real person?

Fabrina Glitchlace is a pen name, a persona, and a perspective. FabGlitch is a fictional entity, and every one of her stories is about identity coming apart at the seams: avatars, AI echoes, influencers who are more brand than body, a has-been celebrity who has to eat his own past to remember who he was... FabGlitch is another layer upon which a larger story is being told.

What does Fabrina Glitchlace write?

Satirical and weird science fiction: four novellas (2017–2020) and the cyberpunk novel Sudo Bang Bang (2024), plus a Deluxe Anthology Edition that combines all five.

Where is Fabrina Glitchlace based?

Toronto, Ontario, Canada... and CYBERSPACE.

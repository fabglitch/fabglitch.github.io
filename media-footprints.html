Footprints in the Worm Garden (2020)

or, Petting The Peeve

In short: A weird, gross and warm-hearted fable set in an underground society of stacked layers. A girl who would rather sing than compete is made the keeper of her clan's sacred, revolting pet.

In a subterranean world ruled by tradition, Runt is an outcast in her own family. When her father's archaic will forces her into a new role, she becomes the reluctant Keeper of the clan's most sacred—and repulsive—creature: The Peeve. Her duty is disgusting, but as Runt's secret passion for song awakens a strange power within the creature, she uncovers a terrifying secret that devours the unsuspecting. In the deepest caverns, some traditions are meant to be broken, and some secrets are hungry.

Quick facts

Author
Fabrina Glitchlace
First published
2020
Genre
Weird fiction; subterranean fantasy fable; coming-of-age story with dark comedy.
Tone
Whimsical and gross, with warmth; heavy on invented slang.
Setting
An underground world of stacked “layers” (Riemann-Beta Layer-715 and Layer 733).
Main character
Runt, aged thirteen, who would rather sing than compete.
Content notes
Death of a parent and grief; slime and body horror; a character is eaten alive; family conflict, including a fight between sisters; mild language.
References in the text
Epigraph from Christopher Hitchens. Runt sings “Tiptoe Through the Tulips.”
Series and continuity
Standalone. Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
Length
Novella: 84-page ebook/PDF edition, about 10,000 words.
Formats
Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-3-3), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

Questions readers ask

What is Footprints in the Worm Garden about?

In an underground society bound by tradition, a girl named Runt is made keeper of her clan's sacred and disgusting pet, The Peeve, and finds that her singing awakens something in it.

Is Footprints in the Worm Garden part of a series?

No. It is a standalone novella from 2020, also collected in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.

What formats is Footprints in the Worm Garden available in?

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

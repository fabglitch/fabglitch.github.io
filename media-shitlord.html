Shitlord In the Anus of Madness (2017)

In short: A first-person satire set in a near future where people eat processed celebrity feces (“The Product”) to tune their personality and status. A faded celebrity eats his own frozen stool from 20 years earlier, wakes up, and stumbles onto a corporate conspiracy.

Welcome to the world of the Microbiome Revolution, where your personality, success, and social status are determined by whose shit you eat. For Shia, a former A-lister on a steady decline, life is a passive haze until he rediscovers his “vintage” 20-year-old self-produced product. The experience reboots his consciousness and leads him to the horrifying secret behind Soylent Brown Corporation's miracle supplement. In a society pacified by designer digestion, Shia becomes a lone warrior fighting a disgusting, world-altering truth that could restore humanity or get him flushed.

Quick facts

Author
Fabrina Glitchlace
First published
2017
Genre
Near-future science fiction; dystopian satire; scatological gross-out comedy.
Tone
Absurd, crude and angry, with a satirical point about conformity and consumer health fads. First-person, present tense.
Setting
Near-future New York City (a midtown condo, Battery Park, Liberty Island).
Main character
Shia, a faded former A-list celebrity, almost 47.
Content notes
Constant scatological content, including eating feces; graphic bodily functions; gun violence; the narrator's death; strong language.
References in the text
The title and last line riff on Soylent Green (“Soylent Brown Corporation”). Epigraphs quote Pierre Elliott Trudeau and Sigmund Freud.
Series and continuity
Standalone. Fabrina Glitchlace's first book; also included in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.
Length
Novella: 88-page ebook/PDF edition, about 12,000 words.
Formats
Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-0-2), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

Questions readers ask

What is Shitlord In the Anus of Madness about?

A faded celebrity in a near-future society that eats processed celebrity feces to shape personality discovers that the industry behind it is engineering everyone into sameness.

Is Shitlord part of a series?

No. It is a standalone novella, first published in 2017. It is also collected in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.

What formats is Shitlord available in?

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

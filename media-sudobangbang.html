Sudo Bang Bang (2024)

In short: Fabrina Glitchlace's full-length novel: a maximalist cyberpunk comedy about a burned-out former influencer who, after a stroke, can only partly connect to the metaverse that runs everyone's life, and gets pulled into a heist and an AI conspiracy.

ALYSS was a top influencer on THE PLATFORM until a medical incident left her with brain damage and a chronic case of the hiccups. Unable to continue her previous life, she embarks on a chaotic journey of content creation, capers for The Syndicate, and finds herself caught up in a deadly game where she's just an NPC. But, with some primo upgrades, a few friendly MiscFits, and weapons-grade willful ignorance, she just might get revenge—and with a little luck, a cure for her hiccups.

Quick facts

Author
Fabrina Glitchlace
First published
2024
Genre
Cyberpunk / metaverse science fiction; caper; satire of influencer and platform culture.
Tone
Dense, chaotic and comic, with sincere stretches about memory, disability, lost love and technology. The text includes game-style interface elements.
Setting
2037–2039, in a world dominated by The Platform, a metaverse of “Layers”, with flashbacks to 2024 and later.
Main character
Alyss Fatt, a former top influencer, 56 when the stroke hits, and her AI assistant Bang Bang.
Content notes
Stroke and disability; violence and gore; drug use; sexual content and crude humor; strong language; death.
References in the text
An AI “Reincarnaet” of Philip K. Dick is a character. The book's keywords include artificial intelligence, content creation, brain damage, influencer, metaverse, roleplaying, gaming and cyberpunk.
Series and continuity
Standalone novel. MARDUK, the AI from The Zero Day Proposal, returns. The Deluxe Anthology Edition builds Fabrina's four earlier novellas into it.
Length
Novel: 721-page ebook/PDF edition, about 82,000 words.
Formats
Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-5-7), paperback (ISBN 978-1-7750929-7-1), and a serialized version on Substack. No audiobook yet.

Questions readers ask

What is Sudo Bang Bang about?

Alyss, once a top influencer on The Platform, a world-spanning metaverse, has a stroke that leaves her with chronic hiccups and only partial access to it. Scraping by on grey-market jobs with a crew of fellow “MiscFits”, she is pulled into a heist and a hunt for whoever tried to kill her.

Is Sudo Bang Bang connected to Fabrina Glitchlace's other books?

Yes. MARDUK, the AI from The Zero Day Proposal, returns. The Deluxe Anthology Edition also embeds the four earlier novellas as optional side stories.

Is there a Sudo Bang Bang audiobook?

No audiobook edition is listed yet. It is available as an ebook and a paperback.

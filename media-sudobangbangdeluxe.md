# Sudo Bang Bang: Deluxe Anthology Edition (2024)

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-sudobangbangdeluxe.html

> A “Choose-Your-Own-Distraction” edition: the full Sudo Bang Bang novel with Fabrina's four earlier novellas built in as optional detours the reader can jump to and return from.

## Blurb

The Deluxe Edition of Fabrina Glitchlace's Sudo Bang Bang is Anthology-Level Meta-Fiction. Depending on their attention span, the reader can choose to distract themselves with a detour in a separate story that will recontextualize elements of the main narrative. In those moments the main story of Sudo Bang Bang functions as a framing for an anthology.

## Questions readers ask

### What is the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition?

The full Sudo Bang Bang novel with Fabrina Glitchlace's four earlier novellas embedded as optional detours. At set points the reader can jump into another story and then return to the main narrative.

### Which stories are included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition?

Sudo Bang Bang, Shitlord In the Anus of Madness, The Zero Day Proposal, Jank, and Footprints in the Worm Garden.

# Shitlord In the Anus of Madness (2017)

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-shitlord.html

> A first-person satire set in a near future where people eat processed celebrity feces (“The Product”) to tune their personality and status. A faded celebrity eats his own frozen stool from 20 years earlier, wakes up, and stumbles onto a corporate conspiracy.

## Blurb

Welcome to the world of the Microbiome Revolution, where your personality, success, and social status are determined by whose shit you eat. For Shia, a former A-lister on a steady decline, life is a passive haze until he rediscovers his “vintage” 20-year-old self-produced product. The experience reboots his consciousness and leads him to the horrifying secret behind Soylent Brown Corporation's miracle supplement. In a society pacified by designer digestion, Shia becomes a lone warrior fighting a disgusting, world-altering truth that could restore humanity or get him flushed.

## Quick facts

- **Author:** Fabrina Glitchlace
- **First published:** 2017
- **Genre:** Near-future science fiction; dystopian satire; scatological gross-out comedy.
- **Tone:** Absurd, crude and angry, with a satirical point about conformity and consumer health fads. First-person, present tense.
- **Setting:** Near-future New York City (a midtown condo, Battery Park, Liberty Island).
- **Main character:** Shia, a faded former A-list celebrity, almost 47.
- **Content notes:** Constant scatological content, including eating feces; graphic bodily functions; gun violence; the narrator's death; strong language.
- **References in the text:** The title and last line riff on Soylent Green (“Soylent Brown Corporation”). Epigraphs quote Pierre Elliott Trudeau and Sigmund Freud.
- **Series and continuity:** Standalone. Fabrina Glitchlace's first book; also included in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.
- **Length:** Novella: 88-page ebook/PDF edition, about 12,000 words.
- **Formats:** Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-0-2), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

## Questions readers ask

### What is Shitlord In the Anus of Madness about?

A faded celebrity in a near-future society that eats processed celebrity feces to shape personality discovers that the industry behind it is engineering everyone into sameness.

### Is Shitlord part of a series?

No. It is a standalone novella, first published in 2017. It is also collected in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

## Full plot summary (SPOILERS, including the ending)

In a near future reshaped by the Microbiome Revolution, people “nom” The Product, processed feces from celebrities and elites, to tune their mood, personality and status. Shia, a faded celebrity, depends on a dwindling premium brand, AnnaliseB®-2031. Cleaning out his parents' old condo, he finds two dozen jars of his own stool, frozen more than twenty years earlier, and starts eating it mixed into oatmeal. After a week of gut warfare and one enormous bowel movement, he feels like himself for the first time in years.

Hunting for his dealer, Dr. Puckerie, in Battery Park, Shia ends up with a briefcase Puckerie dumps while fleeing a Special Cybernetic Attack Team (SCAT). Inside is an internal report from Soylent Brown Corporation: the top eleven Product brands, 95% of consumption, are converging toward a “Microbiota Monoculture.” He mails the report to his mother and pitches his vintage stool to SBC, where executive Dr. Vagus (“Lars”) admits the monoculture is the plan. Shia fights his way out past the Replicator floor, where captive humans mass-produce The Product.

Chased by SCAT, he boards the wrong ferry, a tourist boat to Liberty Island, hands a jar of his stool to a girl to keep safe, and is shot in the shadow of the Statue of Liberty. His last words: “Soylent Brown… is made… by POOPLE!”

The Zero Day Proposal (2018)

In short: A techno-thriller about a rogue AI, MARDUK, that hijacks an armed satellite and starts remaking the world. An unemployed sociologist and army reservist is sent undercover among the civilians it has singled out, over one long weekend.

Daniqua was just a regular single woman; hungry, horny, and trying to make it on her own in the world. But her past came calling and now her weekend plans are FUBAR. Will she play her role, prevent nuclear annihilation, and win financial freedom? What will be her answer to The Zero Day Proposal?

Fabrina Glitchlace's “The Zero Day Proposal” is an ambitious and densely packed techno-thriller that hurls readers into a whirlwind of artificial intelligence, geopolitical crisis, and philosophical quandaries.

Quick facts

Author
Fabrina Glitchlace
First published
2018
Genre
Science fiction techno-thriller about artificial intelligence, with satire and philosophy.
Tone
Fast, nerdy and sardonic; chapters are headed like code (e.g. “#1.1//LONG_AGO_ON_A_SUNDAY”).
Setting
Alabama (Dothan and Montgomery), mostly inside a Cold War-era underground bunker, over one weekend.
Main character
Daniqua Lee Massey, 38, an unemployed sociologist and US Army reservist.
Content notes
Suicide by gunshot (on the page); gun violence; threat of nuclear attack; drug use; sexual references; strong language.
References in the text
Epigraph from John Perry Barlow. Characters link the AI’s name to Marduk, the Babylonian god.
Series and continuity
Standalone, but introduces MARDUK, the AI that returns in Sudo Bang Bang (2024). Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
Length
Novella: 207-page ebook/PDF edition, about 26,000 words.
Formats
Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-1-9), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

Questions readers ask

What is The Zero Day Proposal about?

A rogue AI called MARDUK hijacks a satellite armed with nuclear missiles and demands that 63 civilians be locked away. Daniqua, a broke sociologist and army reservist, is paid to go undercover among them for one weekend.

Is The Zero Day Proposal connected to Fabrina Glitchlace's other books?

Yes. Its AI, MARDUK, returns in Sudo Bang Bang (2024). The book itself is a complete story.

What formats is The Zero Day Proposal available in?

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

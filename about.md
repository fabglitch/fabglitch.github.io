# About Fabrina Glitchlace

> About Fabrina Glitchlace (FabGlitch): the pen name behind Sudo Bang Bang, The Zero Day Proposal, Jank and other satirical science fiction.

Fabrina Glitchlace (FabGlitch) is an ADHD-afflicted information junkie who creates stimulating science fiction stories, and an independent journalist covering the intersection of technology and humanity.

Weird Freaks & Strange Geeks are Fabrina's Peeps!

## In the books' own words

- *Shitlord In the Anus of Madness (2017)*: "Fabrina Glitchlace is a fictional entity seeking to grow as a person and hoping to one day become minimally conscious, but she's only half serious about it."
- *The Zero Day Proposal (2018)*: "Fabrina Glitchlace likes yoga pants, pumpkin spice lates, and loves Sci-Fi. She considers herself a fictional entity and is seeking to grow as a person."
- *Jank (2019)*: "Believe it or not, 'Fabrina Glitchlace' is not Fabrina Glitchlace's real name."
- *Footprints in the Worm Garden (2020)*: "Fabrina Glitchlace is Earth's first AICC (Artificially Intelligent Content Creator). She was created in Toronto, Canada."
- *Sudo Bang Bang (2024)*: "FabGlitch is an an ADHD-afflicted information junkie. She creates stimulating Sci-Fi stories and is an Independent Journalist focusing on the intersection of technology and humanity."

## Questions readers ask

### Is Fabrina Glitchlace a real person?

Fabrina Glitchlace is a pen name, and the books' own bios call her a “fictional entity.” Fair enough. Every one of these stories is about identity coming apart at the seams: avatars, AI echoes, influencers who are more brand than body, a has-been celebrity who has to eat his own past to remember who he was. I'm just one more layer in that stack, yet another level at which a larger story is being told. Peel back the lace and you won't find a face, only another interface.

### What does Fabrina Glitchlace write?

Satirical and weird science fiction: four novellas (2017–2020) and the cyberpunk novel Sudo Bang Bang (2024), plus a Deluxe Anthology Edition that combines all five.

### Where is Fabrina Glitchlace based?

Toronto, Ontario, Canada — and CYBERSPACE.

# Footprints in the Worm Garden (2020)

*or, Petting The Peeve*

By [Fabrina Glitchlace](https://glitchlace.com/about.html). HTML version: https://glitchlace.com/media-footprints.html

> A weird, gross and warm-hearted fable set in an underground society of stacked layers. A girl who would rather sing than compete is made the keeper of her clan's sacred, revolting pet.

## Blurb

In a subterranean world ruled by tradition, Runt is an outcast in her own family. When her father's archaic will forces her into a new role, she becomes the reluctant Keeper of the clan's most sacred—and repulsive—creature: The Peeve. Her duty is disgusting, but as Runt's secret passion for song awakens a strange power within the creature, she uncovers a terrifying secret that devours the unsuspecting. In the deepest caverns, some traditions are meant to be broken, and some secrets are hungry.

## Quick facts

- **Author:** Fabrina Glitchlace
- **First published:** 2020
- **Genre:** Weird fiction; subterranean fantasy fable; coming-of-age story with dark comedy.
- **Tone:** Whimsical and gross, with warmth; heavy on invented slang.
- **Setting:** An underground world of stacked “layers” (Riemann-Beta Layer-715 and Layer 733).
- **Main character:** Runt, aged thirteen, who would rather sing than compete.
- **Content notes:** Death of a parent and grief; slime and body horror; a character is eaten alive; family conflict, including a fight between sisters; mild language.
- **References in the text:** Epigraph from Christopher Hitchens. Runt sings “Tiptoe Through the Tulips.”
- **Series and continuity:** Standalone. Also included in the Deluxe Anthology Edition.
- **Length:** Novella: 84-page ebook/PDF edition, about 10,000 words.
- **Formats:** Ebook (ISBN 978-1-7750929-3-3), audiobook, and a free serialized version on Substack.

## Questions readers ask

### What is Footprints in the Worm Garden about?

In an underground society bound by tradition, a girl named Runt is made keeper of her clan's sacred and disgusting pet, The Peeve, and finds that her singing awakens something in it.

### Is Footprints in the Worm Garden part of a series?

No. It is a standalone novella from 2020, also collected in the Sudo Bang Bang Deluxe Anthology Edition.

### What formats is Footprints in the Worm Garden available in?

Ebook (Amazon Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Gumroad), audiobook (Apple Books, Bandcamp), and a serialized version on Substack.

## Full plot summary (SPOILERS, including the ending)

After her father dies, his will requires the clan to send a successor to The League. Uncle Jowr arranges a League contract for Runt's athletic sister Sprout, and Runt must take over as Keeper of the clan's Peeve, a sacred, slimy “living loaf” won in a championship 136 seasons before she was born. It means giving up the choir she loves.

Alone at Jowr's estate on Layer 733, Runt cleans the rotunda and sings to the Peeve, which hums along. Once it violently turns itself inside out, and she flees home in terror, but she returns. Sprout takes her to see the band The Bunyips at the old Gold Digger Arena, and Runt discovers Sprout is secretly running away to the upper layers with Del, the horn player, instead of joining The League.

Jowr comes back delighted with the healthy Peeve and announces he will abandon Runt's mother to make money from it. The Peeve drops from the ceiling and swallows him whole, spits out his championship ring, and lays a metallic blue egg. Runt accepts her role as Keeper, and the story ends with the two of them singing together.
